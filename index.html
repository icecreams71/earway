THE SHORT VERSION

A little help.

Listen to the notes and name what you hear. Say “repeat” to hear the question again, “skip” to move on, or “stop” to end the session.

How scoring works

Earn up to 100 points for a correct answer, with 20 points off for each wrong attempt or replay (minimum 20). Get 5 correct answers in a level to unlock the next. Your progress stays in this browser on this device.

Before you set off

Connect Bluetooth, allow microphone access, and start a session while parked. Android Chrome speech recognition may require an internet connection; Bluetooth microphone routing and audio quality vary by device. If voice doesn’t work reliably, use the touch answers only when parked.

Privacy & progress

Scores are stored locally; clearing browser data removes them. Speech recognition is handled by your browser and may send audio to its speech provider.
